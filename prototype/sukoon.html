Why it works, and what's not proven

Slow breathing at about 6 breaths a minute switches on the body's rest system and raises heart-rate variability. It has the strongest evidence here.

Listening to the Quran lowered anxiety in most of the 28 trials in a 2018 systematic review, including before surgery, chemotherapy and MRI scans. “Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest.” (13:28)

Binaural tones reduced anxiety with a medium effect in meta-analyses. The tones stop before the recitation, so the Quran is listened to in silence (7:204).

Not proven: no verse or hadith links a frequency to a surah, and claims about 432 Hz, 528 Hz and 7.83 Hz rest on small pilot studies. The surahs are matched to each goal by their meaning.

Sukoon is a wellbeing exercise, not treatment for a mental-health condition.
