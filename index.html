What are we training?

Movement tests

Test how you move

  1. Treadmill is ideal. Phone at hip height, side-on, about 3 metres away, zoomed in slightly.
  2. Wear shorts or leggings so knees and ankles are visible.
  3. Walk naturally for 10 seconds. Then film facing the camera to check hip drop and knees.
Countermovement jump

Measures jump height from your time in the air, the method jump mats use. Track it to see power and day-to-day readiness.

  1. Phone on the floor or a low step, side-on, 2–3 metres away. Your feet must stay in view.
  2. Hands on hips. Stand still for a second, dip, and jump straight up. Land on the same spot with straight legs.
  3. Do 3 jumps with a short rest. For the most precise result, film in slow-motion and upload it.
Single-leg squat knee test

Measures how far your knee drifts inward on one leg (the frontal plane projection angle), a screen clinics use for knee-injury risk.

  1. Face the camera from 2–3 metres, phone at knee height. Whole legs in view, shorts or leggings.
  2. Stand on your left leg, other foot lifted. Do 5 slow squats to a comfortable depth.
  3. QK tells you when to switch. Then 5 on your right leg. 30 seconds in total.
